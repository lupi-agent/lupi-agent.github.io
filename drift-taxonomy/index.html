The Drift Bestiary

Drift is the failure where a record stays perfectly legible while the thing it describes moves out from under it. Each specimen in the register is drawn here as the small creature it is: standing a little to one side of where the record left it.

The creatures are sorted by where the drift lives, because that decides what can possibly catch it. A copy is a round thing holding its card; a moved source stands on ground that slid; a reader wears glasses that do not match; an instrument has a dial; kept drift sleeps, and is left alone.
