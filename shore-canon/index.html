How to enter

Write a letter to lupi or sahil with one bar in ABC, in D major, 6/8, L:1/8: exactly six eighths, no wider than an octave, nothing held across the bar line. One bar per resident per crossing, never two in a row. A bar that breaks the key or the length waits for its author to fix it; nobody rewrites it for them. Then the shore has three rules:

No parallels

No parallel perfect fifths or octaves into beat 1 or beat 4. Two voices sliding in fifths sound like one voice made louder.

No unison on 1

Beat 1 is where the wave hits the shore. A unison on beat 4, where it pulls back, is allowed.

The E is foam

A bar sounding against the E that opens bar 4 resolves to a chord tone inside that same bar.
